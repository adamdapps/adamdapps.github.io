Getting help with Dolce Pod Timer

Dolce Pod Timer is a one-screen brew timer for Nescafé Dolce Gusto pods — no account, no setup. Most questions are answered below; anything else, email us directly.

No sign-in
There's no account to reset, recover, or lose access to.
No sync
Your bar selection resets each time you close the app, by design.
One screen
Everything the app does lives on the single timer screen.

Frequently asked questions

The timer doesn't match how long my machine actually pours

The seven timings in the app were measured directly from a Dolce Gusto machine, bar by bar, rather than calculated from a flat rate. That said, pour time depends on your specific machine's pump and how full the water tank is, so small differences from one machine to the next are expected. Start the timer the moment liquid begins pouring — not when you press the machine's button — for the closest match.

Why does the app show ads?

A small banner ad, provided by Google AdMob, is shown beneath the instructions at the top of the screen. It's there to keep the app free with no purchases or subscriptions, and it never covers or interrupts the timer itself. See the Privacy Policy below for what this involves.

I found a bug, or the timer stopped working correctly

Sorry about that — please email a short description of what happened, and your device model and iOS version if you have them handy, and we'll take a look.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026 · Applies to the Dolce Pod Timer iOS app

Dolce Pod Timer is built to be simple: there's no account, no login, and no data entry of any kind. This page explains the small amount of technical data involved in showing the app's one advertisement, and nothing more — because there isn't anything more to explain.

What we collect directly

Nothing. The app doesn't ask for your name, email, location, or any personal information. It doesn't have a backend server, and nothing you do inside the app — which bar count you tap, when you start or stop the timer — is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere by us.

Advertising, through Google AdMob

The app shows a single banner ad using Google's AdMob service, which keeps Dolce Pod Timer free. To deliver and measure that ad, AdMob's software may collect technical identifiers from your device — such as your device's advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location inferred from IP address, and basic diagnostic data like crash logs.

On iOS specifically

  • The app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission shortly after opening, as required by Apple, before this identifier is used for advertising.
  • You can allow or decline this at the prompt, or change your answer later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Declining doesn't disable or limit the timer in any way — you'll simply see a less personalized ad.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never see or have access to it ourselves. Google's own privacy policy, linked below, covers how AdMob handles this information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, because we don't have any to sell.
  • We don't use any analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools of our own, beyond what AdMob requires to function.
  • We don't share data with anyone other than Google, as described above.

Children's privacy

Dolce Pod Timer isn't directed at children and isn't designed to collect information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll look into it — though as explained above, the app has no mechanism to collect or store personal information in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new effective date above.
